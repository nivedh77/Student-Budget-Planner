# SPENDORA

### Spend Smart. Save Better.

A simple personal budget tracker built by two friends to keep track of daily expenses and manage money better.

---
## About SPENDORA

It started with a simple thought: where does all our money actually go?

As college students, we often spend on little things throughout the day. Food, travel, subscriptions, personal purchases, and everything in between. These expenses might seem small individually, but they add up over time.

We wanted a simple way to track our daily spending, manage our budgets, and understand our spending habits.

So, instead of looking for an existing solution, we decided to build one ourselves.

That's how SPENDORA came to life.

It's a project we originally built for our own everyday use, and we're sharing it in the hope that it might be useful to others too.

## Features

- **Dashboard:** Get an overview of your budget, expenses, and remaining balance.
- **Expense Tracking:** Add daily expenses and manage transactions in one place.
- **Budget Management:** Set monthly budgets and monitor your spending.
- **Categories:** Organise expenses and understand where your money goes.
- **Analytics:** Visualise spending habits through charts and reports.
- **Rewards:** Track progress and make budgeting a little more interesting.
- **Reports:** Keep track of your financial activity.
- **Settings:** Personalise and manage your experience.
